THE GAME

What is Simon Says?

Simon Says is a classic memory game where the computer flashes a sequence of coloured buttons. Your goal is to remember and repeat the exact sequence. Every correct round adds one more colour, making the game progressively harder — how far can your memory take you?

GET STARTED

How to Play

🎮

Press Start Game

Head to the game arena and hit the Start button to begin a new run.

👀

Watch Closely

The board flashes a sequence of colours — pay close attention.

🖱️

Repeat the Sequence

Click the coloured buttons in the exact order they flashed.

📈

Level Up

Every successful round adds a colour and increases your level.

❌

One Mistake Ends It

A single wrong click ends the game — precision matters.

🏆

Chase the High Score

Try to beat your highest score, saved right in your browser.
